About this website

The goal of this website is to provide data and visual artifacts to better understand the city budget. It is created by acouch, is open source, and builds off of previous open source budget tools. The author particpated in the People's Budget Working Group.

About Philly's Budget

The city budget is a year-round process with very limited opportunities for public input. See the People's Budget Office for more information.
